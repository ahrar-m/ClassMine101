## 💡 Concept Preview: Calculus & Velocity

!!! formula "Key Formula: Instantaneous Rate of Change"
    The derivative of a function \( f(x) \) represents the exact slope of the tangent line at any point:
    \[
    f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x + \Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}
    \]

=== "🎥 Animation Preview"

    Our Manim animations illustrate how secant lines approach the tangent line dynamically as \(\Delta x \to 0\).
